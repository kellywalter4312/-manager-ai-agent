# SCHEDULE (example)

Committed as a structural example. Copy this to `SCHEDULE.md` and replace the
contents with your own - `SCHEDULE.md` is gitignored, so your real week never
reaches the repository.

Every section is optional, and so is the whole file. A missing `SCHEDULE.md`
means "no schedule configured", not an error: Alfred says he does not know your
week rather than assuming one.

## IMPORTANT - what this file cannot do

This file describes when you are around. It grants nothing.

There is no capability, permission, tool, specialist or brain setting it can
express, and the type it parses into has no field for one. Writing
`## Permissions` here does exactly as much as writing it in `USER.md`: nothing.

It also schedules nothing. This project has no timer, no daemon and no cron,
and a document about your week must never become the thing that starts a run.
To run the agent unattended you register it with your own scheduler - that
stays a decision you make deliberately.

## IMPORTANT - keep each section to its content

Every line under a recognised heading is read as one statement about your week.
A sentence explaining what a section means becomes a statement too, so put
explanations here in the preamble rather than inside the sections below.

The three recognised headings are `## Working hours`, `## Quiet hours` and
`## Commitments`. Nothing is parsed into an actual time, so "mornings, mostly"
is as valid as "09:00-17:00" - write whatever describes your week honestly.
Any other heading is ignored, so notes to yourself are safe anywhere else.

## Working hours

monday to friday, 09:00-17:00
saturday mornings when something is urgent

## Quiet hours

after 22:00 on weeknights
all day sunday

## Commitments

- tuesday evenings - class
- first monday of the month - team offsite
